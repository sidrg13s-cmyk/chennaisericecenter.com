Troubleshooting

Common Cello Gas Stove Problems We Fix

Real-world issues reported by Chennai households and how our technicians solve them.

🔥 Low Flame on Small & Medium Burners

Food residue and carbon crust block the burner ports, reducing heat output and increasing cooking time.

🔒 Stiff Cello Rotary Gas Knobs

Heat and dried cooking grease dry out the valve lubricant, making the knobs difficult or painful to turn.

🟡 Yellow Smoky Flame Leaving Dark Soot

Air vent obstruction in the venturi mixing tube causes incomplete combustion and black carbon deposits on cookware.

⚡ Auto-Ignition Spark Failure

Food grease coating the ceramic spark pin or a worn piezo mechanism stops the spark from lighting the gas.

🔴 Gas Odour Near Rubber Pipe Connection

Worn O-rings or a loose hose clamp at the 360-degree swivel inlet nozzle cause minor LPG leakage.

Why Professional Gas Stove Deep Cleaning Matters

Daily cooking spills build up carbon crust inside the burner mixing tube and around the brass nozzles. This restricts gas outflow, causing uneven yellow flames that blacken cookware and waste up to 25% of your LPG fuel. Customer FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions — Cello Gas Stove Repair

Common questions from Chennai homeowners about repair charges, parts, and service timings.

❓ Why is my Cello gas stove flame burning yellow and creating soot?

A yellow flame indicates incomplete combustion due to blocked burner holes or grease inside the venturi mixing tube. Our technician cleans the ports and adjusts the air intake to restore a clean blue flame.

❓ Can a tight or jammed Cello gas stove knob be repaired without replacement?

Yes, in most cases our technician can dismantle the valve cock, clean out dried grease, and apply high-temperature valve lubricant to restore smooth rotation.

❓ How much does it cost to service a Cello 3-burner gas stove in Chennai?

Doorstep inspection is ₹199. A complete 3-burner deep clean and jet calibration typically costs between ₹549 and ₹799.

❓ Do you service Cello gas stoves across all Chennai areas?

Yes, our technicians cover all areas across Chennai including Thoraipakkam, Perungudi, Anna Nagar, Velachery, and OMR.

❓ Why does my Cello stove knob spin freely without turning on the gas?

The inner D-shaped plastic socket of the knob has cracked from heat. Replacing it with a reinforced heat-resistant knob solves the problem immediately.

❓ Can you fix Cello auto-ignition stoves that are not sparking?

Yes, our technicians check and replace cracked ceramic electrodes, piezo hammer units, and battery pulse boxes on Cello models.

❓ Is it safe to continue using a gas stove if there is a mild LPG smell?

No. Turn off the cylinder regulator immediately, ventilate the room, and call our technicians for a safety pressure test.

❓ What warranty is provided on replaced Cello gas stove spare parts?

We offer a 90-day replacement warranty on all functional spare parts replaced during service.

❓ Can a Cello LPG stove be converted to work with piped gas (PNG)?

Yes, our technician replaces the brass injector nozzles with PNG-rated jets and adjusts the air shutters for optimal PNG combustion.

❓ How often should a Cello gas stove be professionally serviced?

We recommend professional servicing once every 6 to 12 months to maintain proper combustion, safety, and gas economy.
