Diagnostics & Repair

Common gas stove Problems We Fix in Chennai

We solve burner, valve, and ignition faults safely and promptly at your residence.

🔥 Burner Not Lighting / No Flame

Spilled milk, tea, or sambar frequently seeps into the brass burner holes or clogs the miniature gas injector nozzle beneath the burner base. We clear carbon blockages and restore gas flow.

🟡 Low or Uneven Yellow Flame

A yellow lazy flame leaves black soot on vessels and wastes fuel. It indicates an incorrect air-to-gas ratio in the mixing tube or partial nozzle clogging. Technicians clean venturi passages and calibrate air rings.

🔘 Stiff, Jammed or Loose Knobs

Knobs that become impossible to turn or spin freely without controlling flame have seized brass valve spindles or cracked internal knob stems. We dismantle, lubricate with high-heat silicone grease, or fit new knobs.

⚡ Auto-Ignition Spark Failure

In battery-operated or piezoelectric ignition gas stoves, the ceramic spark pin can crack, misalign, or suffer from rusted earthing contact. We re-gap spark electrodes or replace faulty piezoelectric pulsers.

💨 Hissing Sound or Odor Diagnosis

Technicians use specialized bubble leak detectors on rubber hose joints, internal brass manifold tubes, and gas cock valves to identify loose clamp connections and ensure complete safety.

🛡️ Glass Top Care & Pan Support Wobble

Unstable vessels tipping over due to warped pan supports or loose spill tray screws can pose kitchen hazards. We replace heavy-duty cast iron or enameled trivets and rubber mounting pads.

Common Questions

Gas Stove Repair FAQs in Chennai

Helpful answers about gas stove troubleshooting, inspection charges, spare parts, and doorstep repair across Chennai.

Why is my gas stove flame burning yellow and staining cookware with soot?
A yellow or orange flame indicates incomplete gas combustion caused by blocked burner ports or grease buildup inside the venturi mixing tube. When the air-fuel ratio is disturbed, unburned carbon deposits form on your cooking pots. Our technician dismantles the burner assembly, cleans the mixing chamber, and adjusts the air shutter to restore a clean, high-efficiency blue flame.
Can a tight or jammed gas stove knob be repaired without buying a new stove?
Yes. Over months of cooking, heat and spilled oil enter the brass rotary gas cock valve, drying out the lubricant and causing the spindle to bind. Our technician opens the valve assembly, cleans out hardened residue, and applies specialized heat-resistant valve grease so the knob rotates smoothly again.
What is the inspection charge for gas stove repair in Chennai?
Our standard doorstep inspection charge is ₹199 across Chennai. The technician examines the stove, tests for gas leaks, and provides a clear repair estimate. If you approve the repair, the inspection fee is adjusted against the final service charge.
Why does my auto-ignition gas stove fail to light with the push button?
Auto-ignition failures typically occur due to grease coating the ceramic spark electrode, a cracked ceramic insulator, or a worn-out piezo hammer mechanism. Our technician checks the electrical path, realigns the spark gap to 3.5mm from the burner ring, or replaces defective ceramic pins on-site.
What should I do immediately if I smell gas around my gas stove?
Turn off the cylinder regulator switch or piped gas isolation valve immediately. Open all kitchen windows and doors for ventilation. Do not turn electrical light switches on or off, and do not light matchsticks. Contact our emergency repair service at 8882055269 for an immediate safety leak test.
Can an LPG cylinder stove be converted to piped natural gas (PNG)?
Yes. Piped natural gas operates at a lower delivery pressure than LPG cylinders, requiring larger injector jet diameters. Our technicians replace the brass nozzles with PNG-rated jets and calibrate the air entrainment vents for clean, safe combustion.
Why does the gas stove flame make a loud popping noise when turned off?
A popping noise upon turning off is known as a flashback pop. It happens when the air-gas ratio is overly lean or when the burner cap is not seated flush on the burner cup. Cleaning the venturi tube and properly aligning the burner cap eliminates this issue.
Do you service both glass top and stainless steel gas stoves?
Yes, we service 2-burner, 3-burner, 4-burner, and multi-burner stoves across all formats including toughened glass tops, stainless steel bodies, manual ignition, and battery/piezo auto-ignition systems.

Gas Stove Repair FAQs for Chennai Customers

Clear information on service charges, ignition repairs, and gas safety guidelines.

What is the visiting charge for gas stove repair in Chennai?
Our doorstep visiting and inspection charge is ₹149 to ₹249 depending on locality. Our technician visits your kitchen, inspects the burners, knobs, gas valves, and ignition unit, and gives you a clear quote before starting any work. If you approve the repair, the visiting fee is included in the service cost.
How much does gas stove ignition repair usually cost?
Piezoelectric or battery-operated auto-ignition repair usually costs between ₹250 and ₹550. If only the ceramic spark pin needs realignment or cleaning, the cost is minimal; if the internal spark unit or wiring harness needs replacement, the technician provides a clear spare-part quote.
Why is one of my gas stove burners giving a weak yellow flame?
A weak yellow flame typically happens when boiled liquids (milk, dal, tea) clog the miniature brass nozzle beneath the burner, reducing gas flow and disrupting the air-to-gas ratio. Our technician cleans the burner ports, clears the venturi tube, and replaces the nozzle jet if corroded.
Can you replace a faulty, stiff, or leaking gas valve at home?
Yes, our technicians carry genuine-grade replacement brass rotary valves and high-temperature gas grease. Stiff valves can often be dismantled, cleaned, and lubricated on-site, while damaged or leaking valves are replaced immediately for complete kitchen safety.
How should I care for a toughened glass top gas stove to prevent damage?
Never pour cold water onto the glass when it is hot from cooking, avoid using oversized tandoor or bati cookers that radiate intense heat downward onto the glass, and keep the spill tray seals intact so liquid does not drip onto internal brass parts.
What should I do immediately if I suspect a gas leak in my kitchen?
Immediately shut off the regulator on top of your LPG cylinder. Open all kitchen windows and doors to allow fresh air in. Do NOT touch any electric switches (neither ON nor OFF), do not turn on exhaust fans, and do not light matches. Call our helpline or your LPG distributor emergency service immediately. Never attempt DIY repair on gas leaks.
