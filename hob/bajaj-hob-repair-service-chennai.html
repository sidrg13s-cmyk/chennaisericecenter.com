Diagnostics & Repair

Common Bajaj Hob Issues We Fix in Chennai

Bajaj hob owners across Chennai report specific auto-ignition clicking, low burner flames, and stiff knob issues due to everyday cooking spills.

⚡ Auto-Ignition Spark Failure or Weak Spark

Moisture ingress or a failing pulse generator results in weak yellow sparks that fail to ignite the gas flow.

🟡 Yellow Sooty Flames on Brass Burners

Accumulated carbon soot in the burner crown restricts air-gas mixing, causing dirty yellow flames that blacken pots.

🔒 Tight or Seized Rotary Control Knobs

Prolonged cooking heat dries the high-temperature grease on the internal brass cock spindle, causing the knob to freeze.

🔴 Flame Blows Out on Low Simmer

Partially choked simmer bypass holes in the gas cock cause the flame to extinguish when dialled down to minimum.

⚠️ Gas Smell Around the Knob Base

Worn internal spindle O-rings allow tiny gas quantities to escape past the valve stem when the burner is lit.

🍳 Pan Support Rubbing and Glass Scratches

Worn or missing silicone buffers on the pan support feet cause metal-on-glass friction that can scratch the hob surface.

Bajaj Hob Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Bajaj hob inspection, burner issues, auto-ignition, and doorstep repairs.

Why is my Bajaj hob clicking non-stop without stopping?

Continuous clicking occurs when boiled-over liquid seeps into the microswitch below the knobs, creating an electrical bridge that triggers the pulse box continuously.

Can you fix Bajaj Majesty and Supercook hobs in Chennai?

Yes. Our technicians carry compatible parts for Bajaj Majesty, Supercook, and compact series hobs for same-day doorstep service across Chennai.

Why are my Bajaj hob burners producing yellow flame?

Yellow flame indicates restricted airflow or clogged flame ports caused by accumulated grease and food carbon, preventing complete gas combustion.

How do you fix a stuck or stiff Bajaj hob knob?

We dismantle the brass valve assembly, remove dried sludge and old grease, and lubricate the spindle with specialized high-temperature gas grease.

What is the replacement cost for a Bajaj hob pulse box in Chennai?

Pulse generator replacement generally costs between ₹500 and ₹1,200 depending on whether your model uses an AC mains unit or battery module.

Why does the flame go out when I switch to low flame?

The simmer bypass port inside the gas valve or nozzle is partially blocked with carbon dust, which starves the flame of fuel when dialed down.

Do you provide same-day Bajaj hob repair in Nungambakkam and Ambattur?

Can you replace a broken ceramic spark pin on a Bajaj hob?

Yes, we carry replacement ceramic spark electrodes and calibrate the correct spark gap to ensure instant burner ignition.
