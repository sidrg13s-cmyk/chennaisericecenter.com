Diagnostics & Repair

Common Hindware Hob Issues We Fix in Chennai

Hindware hob owners across Chennai report specific auto-ignition clicking, low burner flames, and stiff knob issues due to everyday cooking spills.

🔴 Flame Shuts Off Immediately on Releasing Knob

Carbon deposits on the Hindware thermocouple pin or a weak magnetic safety solenoid cause the Flame Failure Device (FFD) to cut gas supply as soon as the knob is released.

⚡ Non-Stop Machine-Gun Ignition Spark Clicking

Boiling milk or curry spills run down the knob bezels, creating electrical continuity in the microswitches and keeping the pulse generator firing continuously.

🟡 Low or Uneven Flame on Italian Sabaf Burners

Microscopic flame slots around the Italian Sabaf brass crowns become clogged with oil residue, resulting in irregular yellow flames and cold spots.

🔒 Jammed or Tight Metallic Control Knobs

High radiant heat from heavy woks dries internal factory lubricants within the brass gas cock, making the knob difficult to rotate or completely locked.

⚠️ Mild LPG Odor Behind the Hob Deck

Thermal fatigue or aging manifold gaskets can lead to micro gas leaks beneath the hob chassis, requiring pressure testing and seal replacement.

🍳 Pan Support Imbalance and Surface Wear

Worn silicone buffer pads beneath heavy cast iron trivets allow cookware to wobble and risk scratching or chipping the tempered glass surface.

Hindware Hob Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Hindware hob inspection, burner issues, auto-ignition, and doorstep repairs.

Why does my Hindware hob flame go off as soon as I release the knob?

This is the most common issue on Hindware hobs equipped with Flame Failure Devices (FFD). It happens when carbon coats the thermocouple probe tip or when the magnetic safety solenoid fails to generate enough holding force.

Can you service Italian Sabaf burners on Hindware hobs in Chennai?

Yes. Our technicians are experienced with genuine Italian Sabaf burner assemblies and carry compatible burner crowns, thermocouples, and nozzles.

Why is my Hindware hob making continuous clicking sounds?

Continuous clicking happens when liquid overflow enters the microswitch behind the knobs, keeping the electronic spark circuit permanently closed.

How do you fix a jammed or stiff Hindware hob knob?

We dismantle the brass valve, dissolve old hardened grease, polish the spindle, and reassemble with high-temperature silicone gas grease.

What is the replacement cost for a Hindware thermocouple sensor in Chennai?

Thermocouple sensor replacement typically costs between ₹550 and ₹1,100 depending on the model and length of the probe lead.

Why are the flames on my Hindware Sabaf burner yellow and uneven?

Yellow flame indicates restricted airflow or clogged flame ports caused by accumulated grease and food carbon, preventing complete gas combustion.

Do you provide same-day Hindware hob repair in Alwarpet and Adyar?

Yes, we provide same-day doorstep service across Alwarpet, Adyar, RA Puram, Anna Nagar, Velachery, and all Chennai localities.

How long does a Hindware hob service visit usually take?

Standard hob repairs take about 45 to 60 minutes, including complete safety testing and flame calibration.

Can a cracked glass top on a Hindware hob be replaced?

Cracked toughened glass cannot be repaired and must be replaced as a complete top assembly for safety. We inspect the frame and advise on part availability.
