Diagnostics & Repair

Common Adfresh Hob Issues We Fix in Chennai

Adfresh hob owners across Chennai report specific auto-ignition clicking, low burner flames, and stiff knob issues due to everyday cooking spills.

⚡ Battery Auto-Ignition Spark Failure

Corroded battery contacts in the bottom tray or a dead pulse generator box prevent the ignition spark from reaching the burner cap.

🟡 Yellow Smoky Flame on Brass Burners

Food residue encrusts the brass burner slots, restricting primary air mixing and causing soot on cookware.

🔒 Stiff or Jammed Rotary Knobs

Cooking heat dries out the factory grease inside the brass gas cock, making the knob tight or completely jammed.

🔴 Flame Cuts Off on Simmer Heat

Choked micro simmer holes inside the brass valve starve the burner of fuel when adjusted to low heat.

⚠️ Minor Gas Smell at Swivel Connection

Hardened sealing gaskets on the rear gas inlet joint cause minor gas seepage behind the countertop.

🍳 Wobbly Vessel Pan Trivets

Worn or missing silicone feet under the pan support allow hot vessels to wobble and scratch the glass top.

Helpful Answers

Adfresh Hob Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Adfresh hob inspection, burner issues, auto-ignition, and doorstep repairs.

Why is my Adfresh hob not sparking even with a new battery?

If a new battery does not help, the battery holder terminals may be corroded with acid, the pulse box inside may have failed, or the microswitch under the knob is not closing the circuit.

Do you service Adfresh hobs across Chennai?

Yes, our technicians carry compatible spares for Adfresh glass built-in and tabletop hobs for same-day doorstep service across Chennai.

How often should an Adfresh kitchen hob undergo deep cleaning?

For regular South Indian cooking involving tadka and frying, deep cleaning every 6 to 9 months keeps burner holes clear and prevents sticky knob mechanisms.

Can you fix uneven half-circle flame on an Adfresh brass burner?

Yes. We remove the brass burner caps and crowns, decarbonize blocked flame slots, and clear the jet nozzle to restore a complete 360-degree blue flame.

What is the indicative cost to replace an Adfresh hob pulse generator?

Replacing an Adfresh battery pulse generator box generally costs between ₹420 and ₹950 including labor and parts.

Why does my Adfresh hob produce yellow flame and black soot?

Yellow flame indicates incomplete combustion, usually caused by grease clogging the venturi mixing tube or carbon deposits blocking the burner crown holes.

Do you fix gas leaks on Adfresh hobs in Chennai?

Yes. Our technicians use bubble test leak detectors to trace gas leaks at the swivel nozzle, internal manifold, or valve seals and replace defective gaskets.

How do I know if my Adfresh hob knob needs replacement?

If the knob feels loose, slips on the shaft, or turns without opening the gas valve, the inner plastic D-ring has cracked and needs replacement.
