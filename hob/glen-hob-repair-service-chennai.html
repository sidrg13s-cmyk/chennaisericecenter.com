Diagnostics & Repair

Common Glen Hob Issues We Fix in Chennai

Glen hob owners across Chennai report specific auto-ignition clicking, low burner flames, and stiff knob issues due to everyday cooking spills.

⚡ Non-Stop Machine-Gun Ignition Clicking

Liquid boiling over the knob bezels leaks into the Italian microswitch harness, triggering continuous pulse firing that drains batteries or risks pulse coil burnout.

🟡 Yellow Inefficient Flame on Forged Brass Burners

Accumulated tadka oil and carbon dust choke the precision slots of Glen forged brass burner rings, reducing heat and dirtying cookware.

🔒 Stiff or Seized Italian Brass Valve Spindles

High thermal radiation from heavy cast iron cookware dries factory silicone lubricants, making Glen knobs stiff and prone to cracking internal D-rings.

🔴 Flame Failure Sensor Extinguishing Burner

On Glen models with Flame Failure Devices (FFD), soot accumulation on the thermocouple probe prevents the electromagnetic valve from holding gas open.

🍳 Pan Trivet Wobbling on Beveled Glass

Missing or heat-perished heat-resistant silicone feet on heavy cast iron trivets scratch the beveled glass edge and destabilize heavy vessels.

⚠️ Subtle Gas Odor Around Manifold Couplings

Vibration and thermal cycling can loosen Glen internal manifold screws or perish inlet O-rings, leading to mild gas leakage under the hob.

Glen Hob Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Glen hob inspection, burner issues, auto-ignition, and doorstep repairs.

Why is my Glen hob clicking continuously without stopping?

Continuous clicking usually happens when liquid from boiled over milk, sambar, or tea seeps into the microswitches under the control knobs, causing an electrical bridge that triggers the pulse box repeatedly.

Do you service Glen 1074 and 1073 forged brass hobs in Chennai?

Yes. Our technicians service Glen 1074, 1073, 1065, and compact series hobs with compatible Italian-spec parts across all Chennai areas.

What causes weak yellow flame on Glen forged brass burners?

Yellow flame occurs when burner flame ports or the gas injector nozzle become clogged with oil and food residue, disturbing the air-gas ratio and causing incomplete combustion.

Can you fix a jammed or stiff Glen hob knob?

Yes. We dismantle the Italian rotary brass gas valve, remove hardened old grease and carbon deposits, and lubricate the spindle with high-temperature gas grease.

How much does Glen hob pulse box replacement cost in Chennai?

Electronic pulse box replacement typically costs between ₹650 and ₹1,450 depending on whether it is a battery-operated or AC mains-powered model.

Why does the flame go off as soon as I release the Glen knob?

On models with Flame Failure Devices (FFD), this happens if the thermocouple tip is covered with carbon or if the magnetic valve is weak and fails to hold gas open.

Do you provide doorstep Glen hob repair in Kotturpuram and ECR?

Yes, we provide same-day doorstep service across Kotturpuram, ECR, Besant Nagar, Adyar, Velachery, and all Chennai localities.

How long does a typical Glen hob service visit take?

Most hob repairs—including burner cleaning, spark electrode replacement, or valve lubrication—are completed within 45 to 60 minutes at your residence.

What is your doorstep visiting charge for Glen hob inspection?

Our inspection charge is ₹249 to ₹349. If you proceed with the repair work, the inspection charge is adjusted into your final bill.

Can scratched or cracked beveled glass on a Glen hob be repaired?

Cracked toughened glass cannot be repaired and must be replaced as a complete top assembly for safety. We inspect the frame and advise on part availability.
