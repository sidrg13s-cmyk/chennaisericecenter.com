Koryo Microwave Oven Repair Experience

Koryo 28L Convection Microwave Oven ⭐ 10/10

Complaint: "Microwave on aaguthu, plate rotate aagudhu, but food hot-eh aaga maattenguthu."

Technician Checked: Magnetron tube filament resistance, high voltage capacitor, and HV diode.

Fault & Fix: High voltage diode had shorted and magnetron vacuum tube had lost emission. Discharged HV capacitor safely, installed new magnetron with thermal paste, and tested heat test with 500ml water.

Approx. Cost: ₹1,400 – ₹2,600 📍 Velachery
Koryo 20L Solo Microwave Oven ⭐ 9/10

Complaint: "Inside cooking chamber-la sparks and fire crackling sound varuthu, light flash aagudhu."

Technician Checked: Mica waveguide cover sheet, inner cavity paint, and stirrer blade.

Fault & Fix: Mica waveguide sheet was charred and saturated with food oil spatter. Removed burnt mica sheet, degreased chamber surface, installed custom-cut new mica card.

Approx. Cost: ₹400 – ₹750 📍 Porur
