Why Regular Care Is Important for Your Faber Microwave

Taking proper care of your Faber microwave oven ensures long-lasting performance and keeps your kitchen safe in Chennai. Always wipe down food splatters from interior walls and the mica waveguide cover immediately, as burnt food particles create electrical arcing that damages the magnetron. Avoid operating the microwave completely empty, and only use certified microwave-safe glassware or ceramics. Keep enough space around the top and rear vents so internal cooling fans can release heat easily. If you notice persistent humming or sluggish heating, booking an inspection early prevents costly transformer or control board failure. Practicing simple daily kitchen maintenance protects your appliance investment for years.

Frequently Asked Questions About Faber Repair in Chennai

Helpful answers regarding doorstep checking, repairs, spare parts, and visiting charges in Chennai.

Why is my Faber microwave oven turning on and counting down but food is not heating in Chennai?

Microwave heat aagala? When the oven runs but doesn't heat food, the magnetron tube, high-voltage diode, or capacitor has likely failed. Our Chennai technician tests high-voltage circuits and replaces defective heating components on-site.

Why does my Faber microwave trip the house MCB switch when the door is opened or closed?

This happens when door interlock safety microswitches get short-circuited or misaligned. We realign the door latch hooks and replace worn microswitches.

Do you service solo, grill, and convection Faber microwaves in Chennai?

Yes, we repair all types including solo, grill, and convection microwave ovens across Chennai localities like Velachery, Porur, Anna Nagar, and Adyar.

Why are there sparks and crackling sounds inside my Faber microwave cavity?

Sparking inside the cooking cavity is almost always caused by a burnt mica waveguide cover sheet that has absorbed food splatter. We clean the chamber, remove carbon burns, and install a new mica card.

Why is the glass turntable tray inside my Faber microwave not rotating?

A broken synchronous turntable motor or worn drive coupler prevents the plate from turning. Replacing the small drive motor restores smooth rotation.

How much does magnetron or diode replacement cost for a Faber microwave in Chennai?

Indicative pricing in Chennai: a high-voltage diode replacement is ₹350 to ₹650, while a genuine-grade magnetron replacement ranges between ₹1,300 and ₹2,600.

Why are touch buttons on my Faber microwave oven not responding?

Kitchen moisture and steam can oxidize copper tracks on the touch membrane keypad. Our technician tests flat cable connections and replaces the touch membrane panel.
