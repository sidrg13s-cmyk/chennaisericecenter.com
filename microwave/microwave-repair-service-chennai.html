Microwave Problems We Repair in Chennai

If your microwave oven displays any of these common failure symptoms, our Chennai technicians can check and fix it safely at your doorstep.

Microwave Not Heating Food The oven turns on, light glows, and turntable spins, but food stays cold. This points to a faulty high-voltage diode, capacitor, or burnt magnetron tube.

Sparking Inside the Cavity Visible sparks and crackling sounds during operation. Usually caused by grease-damaged mica waveguide cover sheets or scratched cavity paint.
🔄
Turntable Plate Not Rotating The glass tray does not spin, leading to uneven cooking. Caused by a stripped drive coupler, damaged roller ring, or burnt synchronous motor.
🔌
Microwave Not Turning On (Dead Set) No display light, no beep sound, and zero response when plugged in. We inspect the main power fuse, thermal cut-off switch, and power cord.
🔘
Touch Keypad Not Responding Buttons fail to register touches or certain numbers cannot be pressed. Caused by moisture corrosion or wear on the membrane keypad ribbon cable.
🚪
Door Latch & Switch Issues Microwave will not start or stops when the door is touched. Caused by misaligned door hooks or failing primary and secondary safety micro-switches.
🔊
Loud Humming or Buzzing Noise Abnormally loud vibrating sound during heating. Points to high-voltage transformer stress, cooling fan obstruction, or diode failure.
⚠️
Tripping Home Power / MCB Starting the microwave immediately trips the room circuit breaker. Indicates an internal direct short circuit in the door switch or high-voltage circuit.
🍗
Grill Element Not Heating Normal microwave mode works, but grill function does not brown food. We check the quartz heating tubes and control board grill power relays.
🎂
Convection Oven Not Reaching Temperature Cakes and baked dishes do not cook properly in convection mode. We inspect the circular heating element, thermostat, and convection blower fan.
💨
Burning Smell or Smoke Inside Burnt plastic or electrical smell when running. Caused by overheated transformer windings, food buildup behind waveguide, or fan motor failure.
⏱️
Stops After Few Seconds of Running The oven starts cooking and cuts off after 3 to 10 seconds. Usually triggered by inverter board protection, faulty thermal sensors, or cooling failure.

Microwave Parts We Repair or Replace in Chennai

We inspect and service the following components where applicable. Component repair or replacement depends on the exact fault, condition, and model.

⚡ Magnetron Tube

The core electromagnetic generator tube that produces cooking waves. Tested for open filaments, burnt antenna caps, and internal shorts.

🔋 High-Voltage Capacitor

Stores high electrical charge to double voltage for the magnetron. Tested with capacitance meters and safely discharged before service.

🔌 High-Voltage Diode

Converts AC voltage into high DC voltage for heating. Replaced when shorted, open, or causing high-voltage fuse failure.

🛡️ Mica Waveguide Cover

Protects the magnetron antenna from food splatters and steam. Replaced when burned or carbonized to eliminate dangerous cavity sparking.

🔄 Turntable Synchronous Motor

Rotates the glass plate at steady RPM for even cooking. Replaced when gears strip, coils burn, or rotation stutters.

🚪 Door Safety Interlock Switches

Primary, secondary, and monitor micro-switches that stop operation when door opens. Replaced when contacts burn or stick.

🖥️ Electronic Control Board (PCB)

Coordinates cooking timers, power levels, and relays. Repaired at component level or replaced for relay and power supply faults.

🔘 Touch Keypad Membrane

Flexible conductive keypad on the front panel. Cleaned, serviced, or replaced when buttons refuse to register touches.

🌬️ Cooling & Convection Fans

Exhausts hot air from the magnetron and circulates heat in convection mode. Serviced or replaced for quiet, reliable airflow.

🍗 Grill & Convection Elements

Quartz and sheath heating elements for browning and baking. Tested for resistance continuity and replaced when open-circuited.

🌡️ Thermal Fuse & Thermostats

Safety temperature cut-offs mounted on magnetron and cavity. Replaced when tripped to restore normal power connectivity.

💡 Interior Light & Glass Plate

Cavity illumination lamps, glass turntables, and roller support rings replaced for clear visibility and smooth tray motion.

Common Signs Your Microwave Needs Repair

Watch out for these early warning signs to avoid dangerous electrical shorts or complete appliance breakdown.

⚠️ Cold Food After Cooking The light turns on and tray turns, but dishes remain stone cold after several minutes. Indicates high-voltage diode, capacitor, or magnetron failure.
⚠️ Visible Sparking or Flash Crackling blue or yellow sparks inside the right wall indicate carbonized food on the mica sheet. Stop using immediately to prevent magnetron damage.
⚠️ Jerky or Stationary Glass Plate If the tray wobbles, grinds, or stays stationary, food cooks unevenly with cold spots. Points to a worn coupler or failing turntable motor.
⚠️ Heavy Burning Electrical Smell A sharp burning odor indicates overheated transformer insulation, burnt wiring, or plastic coupler friction. Unplug the microwave safely.
⚠️ Unresponsive Start / Number Keys Touch buttons fail to register or beep without starting. Caused by kitchen steam degrading the conductive membrane tracks on the front control panel.
⚠️ Door Must Be Pushed Hard to Start If the oven only starts when the door is slammed or held, the internal door micro-switches or latch hooks are worn and need realignment.

Why is my microwave oven microwave oven turning on and counting down but food is not heating in Chennai?

Microwave heat aagala? When the oven runs but doesn't heat food, the magnetron tube, high-voltage diode, or capacitor has likely failed. Our Chennai technician tests high-voltage circuits and replaces defective heating components on-site.

Why are touch buttons on my microwave oven microwave oven not responding?

Kitchen moisture and steam can oxidize copper tracks on the touch membrane keypad. Our technician tests flat cable connections and replaces the touch membrane panel.

Why is the glass turntable tray inside my microwave oven microwave not rotating?

A broken synchronous turntable motor or worn drive coupler prevents the plate from turning. Replacing the small drive motor restores smooth rotation.

Why are there sparks and crackling sounds inside my microwave oven microwave cavity?

Sparking inside the cooking cavity is almost always caused by a burnt mica waveguide cover sheet that has absorbed food splatter. We clean the chamber, remove carbon burns, and install a new mica card.

Do you service solo, grill, and convection microwave oven microwaves in Chennai?

Yes, we repair all types including solo, grill, and convection microwave ovens across Chennai localities like Velachery, Porur, Anna Nagar, and Adyar.

How much does magnetron or diode replacement cost for a microwave oven microwave in Chennai?

Indicative pricing in Chennai: a high-voltage diode replacement is ₹350 to ₹650, while a genuine-grade magnetron replacement ranges between ₹1,300 and ₹2,600.

Why does my microwave oven microwave trip the house MCB switch when the door is opened or closed?

This happens when door interlock safety microswitches get short-circuited or misaligned. We realign the door latch hooks and replace worn microswitches.
