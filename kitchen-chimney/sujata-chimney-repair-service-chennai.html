Diagnostics & Repair

Common Sujata Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Sujata chimney owners across Chennai commonly encounter heavy baffle filter grease clogging, motor capacitor microfarad loss, and mechanical switch jamming.

🌪️ Sujata Suction Power Drop

Stainless steel baffle filters become saturated with sticky cooking oil, restricting airflow and forcing kitchen smoke back into living rooms.

🔊 Loud Motor Humming or Bearing Noise

Motor sleeve bearings dry up or the blower wheel gathers uneven grease crusts, creating a loud humming or rattling sound during operation.

🔘 Sujata Push Button Jamming

Mechanical push buttons on Sujata straight line and curved glass models stick inside the bezel due to grease vapor buildup on internal springs.

🔥 Motor Overheating & Thermal Trip

Excessive grease friction on the blower fan causes the motor thermal protector to trip, turning the chimney off unexpectedly.

💧 Oil Dripping from Filter Corners

Liquid grease leaks down from the baffle filter frame onto the cooking counter when internal collection gutters overflow.

⚡ Total Power Failure / Non-Responsive Unit

Power surges or blown glass fuses on the input terminal board render the Sujata chimney completely dead with no lights or motor movement.

Why is my Sujata chimney motor humming but not turning?
This is usually caused by a weakened motor run capacitor or bearings seized by old oil grease. A capacitor replacement or bearing servicing usually resolves the issue without buying a new motor.
Can jammed push buttons on Sujata chimneys be fixed at home?
Yes. Our technician carries compatible 5-key push button assemblies and replaces the damaged switch unit at your doorstep in under an hour.
Why does my Sujata chimney shut down automatically after 10 minutes?
This happens when the motor thermal overload protector trips due to overheating caused by grease-loaded blower blades. Deep degreasing solves this.

Deep chemical degreasing for a Sujata chimney ranges from ₹650 to ₹1,350 depending on chimney size (60cm vs 90cm) and grease thickness.

Why is oil dripping from the corners of my Sujata chimney?
