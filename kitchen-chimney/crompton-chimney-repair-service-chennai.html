Diagnostics & Repair

Common Crompton Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Crompton chimney owners across Chennai commonly encounter blower fan grease imbalance, motor capacitor microfarad loss, and auto-clean heating breaks.

🌪️ Crompton Suction Loss / Smoke Spread

Baffle filters and curved blower impellers choke with sticky cooking grease, reducing suction efficiency and letting kitchen smoke linger.

🔊 QuietPro Blower Vibration & Rattle

Grease accumulates unevenly on the balanced blower blades, causing the QuietPro motor assembly to vibrate loudly at speeds 2 and 3.

🔥 Smart Auto-Clean Heater Failure

The thermal heating element fails to melt internal grease during the auto-clean cycle due to an open circuit in the heating wrap or blown thermal fuse.

🖐️ Gesture & Touch Sensor Unresponsiveness

Touch controls or wave gesture sensors fail to register commands when steam vapors and grease film deposit over the front glass sensor strip.

💧 Oil Dripping Over Kitchen Stove

Grease leaks from the chimney base onto the counter because the internal drainage gutter is choked with hardened cooking oil.

⚡ Motor Humming Without Rotation

The motor makes a continuous humming sound without spinning due to a failed run capacitor or seized shaft bearings.

Crompton Chimney Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Crompton chimney inspection, motor issues, deep cleaning, and doorstep repairs.

What is the visiting charge for Crompton chimney repair in Chennai?
Our doorstep inspection charge is ₹199 to ₹299 across Chennai. This fee is adjusted against the repair charge if you approve the service.
Why is my Crompton chimney making noise despite being a QuietPro model?
QuietPro models stay quiet until grease unbalances the squirrel-cage fan or the motor shaft bearings dry out. Degreasing the fan or replacing worn bearings restores silent performance.
Can stuck push buttons on Crompton chimneys be repaired at home?
Yes. In most cases, we replace the 5-key mechanical switch assembly with a compatible unit at your doorstep in under an hour.
How does Crompton smart auto-clean work and why does it fail?
Smart auto-clean uses a thermal heating wrap to melt oil inside the blower into an oil collector. It fails when the thermal heating element burns out or the thermal fuse blows.
How much does deep cleaning cost for a Crompton chimney in Chennai?
Deep chemical degreasing for a Crompton chimney ranges from ₹650 to ₹1,400 depending on chimney size (60cm vs 90cm) and grease thickness.
Do you carry genuine compatible spares for Crompton chimneys?
Yes, we carry compatible Crompton parts including baffle filters, motor capacitors, auto-clean coils, push switch clusters, and LED drivers.
Why is oil dripping from the corners of my Crompton chimney?
Oil drips when the inner collection canal is choked with solid oil sludge. A thorough canal clean and proper filter alignment stops the drip.
How long does a Crompton chimney motor replacement take?
A complete motor replacement is carried out on-site at your home and takes about 60 to 90 minutes, followed by an airflow and noise test.
