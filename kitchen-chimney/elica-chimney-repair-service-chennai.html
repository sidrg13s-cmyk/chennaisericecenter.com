Diagnostics & Repair

Common Elica Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Elica chimneys in Chennai frequently experience silent motor bearing wear, grease on touch sensors, and auto-clean drainage blocks.

🌪️ Elica Suction Power Drop

Blower fan intake gets choked with sticky oil residues from South Indian seasoning, dropping airflow from 1200 m3/hr to poor venting levels.

🔇 EDS Silence Motor Noise & Grinding

Elica Deep Silence motors develop metallic grinding or rumbling when internal bearings dry out or when grease deposits throw the rotor off balance.

📱 Elica Touch Panel Display Dead / Blinking

Moisture from boiling rasam and hot tadka vapors seeps behind the glass front, causing the digital display to flicker or become completely unresponsive.

🔥 Heat Auto-Clean Heater Inactive

Auto-clean cycle runs its timer but fails to generate heat, leaving the oil collector empty while grease solidifies around the blower casing.

💧 Sticky Grease Dripping Down Front Glass

Internal oil gutter overflows and drips over the curved glass rim when the drainage pitch is blocked by thick black grease crusts.

💡 Halogen / LED Spot Lamp Failure

High ambient heat above gas hobs damages the miniature Elica LED driver circuit, leaving the cooking surface dark.

Elica Chimney Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Elica chimney inspection, motor issues, deep cleaning, and doorstep repairs.

How much is the visiting fee for Elica chimney repair in Chennai?
Our doorstep inspection charge for Elica chimney is ₹199 to ₹299 across Chennai. This visiting charge is adjusted in the final repair bill if you approve the service.
Why is my Elica chimney making noise despite being a Deep Silence model?
Deep Silence models stay quiet until grease unbalances the squirrel-cage fan or the motor shaft bearings dry out. Degreasing the fan or replacing worn bearings restores silent performance.
Can you fix Elica touch panels that stop responding?
Yes. Most Elica touch panel issues are caused by moisture or grease seeping behind the glass. We clean sensor traces or repair the control board without needing full canopy replacement.
What happens if Elica auto-clean doesn’t drain oil into the cup?
This happens either because the heating strip has failed or the internal drain channel is plugged with solid grease. We test the heating coil and clear the drain pipe on-site.
How much does an Elica chimney deep clean cost in Chennai?
A complete Elica chimney deep degreasing service ranges from ₹750 to ₹1,500 depending on model size (60cm vs 90cm) and the level of grease buildup.
Do you provide compatible spares for Elica curved glass chimneys?
Yes, our technicians carry tested compatible Elica spares including capacitors, baffle filters, oil trays, LED drivers, and touch PCB boards.
Why is my Elica chimney blowing air back into the kitchen?
Air backdraft indicates a blocked duct pipe, stuck exterior damper flap, or excessive bends in the aluminum duct. Our technician checks the exhaust run and clears it.
How long does a replacement Elica motor last?
A quality compatible copper winding motor typically lasts 5 to 7 years with regular cleaning and proper duct maintenance.
Do you repair Elica island chimneys in Chennai apartments?
Yes, our technicians have ladders and ceiling mounting tools to service Elica island hoods in flats and villas across Chennai.
