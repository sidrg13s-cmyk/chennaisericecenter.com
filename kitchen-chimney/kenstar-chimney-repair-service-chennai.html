Diagnostics & Repair

Common Kenstar Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Kenstar chimney owners in Chennai frequently encounter baffle filter grease saturation, mechanical push button jamming, and motor capacitor wear.

🌪️ Kenstar Suction Power Drop

Stainless steel baffle filters choke with solidified cooking fat, reducing suction volume and leaving cooking smoke hovering in kitchen rooms.

🔊 Loud Motor Humming or Bearing Noise

Motor sleeve bearings dry up or the blower wheel gathers uneven grease crusts, creating a loud humming or rattling sound during operation.

🔘 Kenstar Push Button Jamming

Mechanical push buttons on Kenstar straight line and curved glass models stick inside the bezel due to grease vapor buildup on internal springs.

🔥 Auto-Clean Heating Element Fault

In Kenstar auto-clean models, the thermal coil stops heating up, preventing grease from melting into the stainless steel oil collector.

💧 Oil Dripping from Filter Corners

Liquid grease leaks down from the baffle filter frame onto the cooking counter when internal collection gutters overflow.

⚡ Total Power Failure / Non-Responsive Unit

Power surges or blown glass fuses on the input terminal board render the Kenstar chimney completely dead with no lights or motor movement.

Kenstar Chimney Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Kenstar chimney inspection, motor issues, deep cleaning, and doorstep repairs.

What is the visiting charge for Kenstar chimney repair in Chennai?
Our doorstep inspection charge is ₹199 to ₹299 across Chennai. If you choose to carry out the recommended repair, this visiting charge is adjusted in the final bill.
Why is my Kenstar chimney motor humming but not turning?
This is usually caused by a weakened motor run capacitor or bearings seized by old oil grease. A capacitor replacement or bearing servicing usually resolves the issue without buying a new motor.
Can jammed push buttons on Kenstar chimneys be fixed at home?
Yes. Our technician carries compatible 5-key push button assemblies and replaces the damaged switch unit at your doorstep in under an hour.
How does Kenstar auto-clean work and why does it fail?
Heat auto-clean uses a heating wrap to melt oil inside the blower into an oil collector. It fails when the thermal heating element burns out or the thermal fuse blows.
How much does deep cleaning cost for a Kenstar chimney in Chennai?
Deep chemical degreasing for a Kenstar chimney ranges from ₹650 to ₹1,350 depending on chimney size (60cm vs 90cm) and grease thickness.
Do you carry genuine compatible spares for Kenstar chimneys?
Yes, we carry compatible Kenstar parts including baffle filters, motor capacitors, auto-clean coils, push switch clusters, and LED drivers.
Why is oil dripping from the corners of my Kenstar chimney?
Oil drips when the inner collection canal is choked with solid oil sludge. A thorough canal clean and proper filter alignment stops the drip.
How long does a Kenstar chimney motor replacement take?
A complete motor replacement is carried out on-site at your home and takes about 60 to 90 minutes, followed by an airflow and noise test.
