Diagnostics & Repair

Common Livpure Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Livpure chimney owners in Chennai frequently encounter auto-clean heating coil breaks, touch sensor unresponsiveness, and blower fan oil saturation.

🔥 Thermal Auto-Clean Heating Failure

The thermal heating element fails to melt internal grease during the cleaning cycle due to open circuit in the heating wrap or damaged PCB relay.

🌪️ Weak Suction on Heavy Cooking

Centrifugal suction drops significantly when the blower impeller blades get coated in heavy fried oil layers, reducing air intake volume.

💧 Oil Leaking from Chimney Base

Cooking oil overflows from internal collection grooves onto the kitchen gas stove because the oil collection tray chute is choked.

🔊 Rattling & Vibration on High Speed

Livpure blower fan wheel develops rotational imbalance when caked grease sticks unevenly to one side of the fan blades.

🖐️ Touch Panel Sensor Freezing

Touch controls fail to trigger speeds or register finger taps due to steam moisture and grease mist penetrating behind the front glass.

⚡ Motor Fails to Spin / Humming

Motor hums but does not start spinning because the run capacitor has failed or motor shaft bearings have seized from oil varnish.

Helpful Answers

Livpure Chimney Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Livpure chimney inspection, motor issues, deep cleaning, and doorstep repairs.

What is the visiting fee for Livpure chimney service in Chennai?
Our doorstep inspection charge is ₹199 to ₹299 across Chennai. This fee is adjusted against the repair charge if you approve the service.
Why is my Livpure chimney auto-clean not melting grease into the oil cup?
This is usually caused by an open thermal fuse, burnt heating element wrap, or a faulty relay on the PCB. Our technician tests and replaces the component on-site.
Can stuck push buttons on Livpure chimneys be repaired?
Yes. In most cases, we replace the 5-key mechanical switch assembly with a compatible unit so you do not have to struggle with jammed buttons.
How often should a Livpure chimney receive deep cleaning in Chennai?
Even with auto-clean, deep manual degreasing is recommended every 6 months to remove hardened oil that heat alone cannot melt.
How much does a Livpure chimney motor replacement cost in Chennai?
A compatible copper winding blower motor for Livpure chimneys generally costs between ₹1,850 and ₹3,450 depending on model wattage and size.
Why does my Livpure chimney vibrate violently on high speed?
Vibration occurs when grease accumulates unevenly on the blower fan wheel blades, throwing off rotational balance. A thorough fan wash fixes this.
Do you carry replacement oil collector cups for Livpure chimneys?
Yes, our technicians carry compatible oil collector trays and cups for standard Livpure models across Chennai.
Why is oil dripping onto my stove from the Livpure chimney?
Oil drips when the inner drainage channel gets blocked with black grease sludge. We clear the internal channel so oil drains properly into the cup.
Can you service Livpure chimneys in South Chennai areas like Tambaram and Medavakkam?
Yes, our technicians cover all localities across South, North, Central, and West Chennai with prompt doorstep visits.
Is it safe to use Livpure chimney if the motor is humming without spinning?
No, switch it off immediately to prevent motor winding burnout. A humming motor usually only needs a new run capacitor to start spinning again.
