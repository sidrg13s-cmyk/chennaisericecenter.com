Common Faber Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Faber chimney owners across Chennai report specific motor vibration, 3D airflow drop, and gesture touch issues due to hot grease vapors.

🌪️ Faber 3D Suction Reduction

Faber three-way suction ports get choked with sticky oil grease after months of fish fry and tempering, choking the air passage and leaving smoke hanging in the kitchen.

🔊 Loud Motor Humming & Whining

Faber heavy-duty suction motor begins whining or vibrating when the fan blower wheel collects thick oil crusts, causing balance loss on the motor shaft.

🖐️ Gesture & Touch Sensor Malfunction

Faber wave-sensor and feather-touch panels fail to register hand motions when kitchen grease film settles over the optical emitter strip behind the front glass.

🔥 Heat Auto-Clean Cycle Not Heating

In Faber auto-clean models, the thermal heating coil may fail to heat up during the 9-minute cycle, leaving the oil collector cup bone dry while grease stays inside.

💧 Oil Dripping Over Faber Glass Canopy

Grease droplets leaking from the lower edge of the Faber tempered glass hood indicate that the inner oil gutter channel is packed with hardened masala grease.

⚡ Faber PCB Power Tripping & Dead Unit

Sudden electrical spikes in Chennai homes can pop the fuse or scorch the capacitor bridge on the Faber main circuit board, causing complete power blackout.

Helpful Answers

Faber Chimney Repair FAQs in Chennai

Common questions regarding Faber chimney inspection, motor issues, deep cleaning, and doorstep repairs.

What is the visiting charge for Faber chimney inspection in Chennai?
Our Faber chimney doorstep inspection fee is ₹199 to ₹299 across Chennai. If you proceed with the suggested repair or part replacement, this visiting fee is adjusted into the final invoice.
Why is my Faber chimney gesture control not responding to hand waves?
Faber gesture control relies on optical infrared sensors. Hot oil steam often leaves a micro-layer of grease on the sensor lenses behind the black glass. Our technician cleans the sensors or replaces the sensor ribbon module if corroded.
How often should a Faber chimney be deep cleaned in Chennai?
For regular Chennai home cooking involving sambar tadka, fish frying, and deep frying, we recommend deep degreasing every 5 to 6 months to prevent oil dripping and motor strain.
Can Faber chimney auto-clean heating issues be repaired at home?
Yes. Our technician tests the heating element, thermal cut-off fuse, and PCB relay on-site at your home and replaces the faulty part in a single visit.
How much does a Faber chimney motor replacement cost in Chennai?
A compatible high-torque copper motor for Faber chimneys typically ranges from ₹1,950 to ₹3,800 depending on whether it is an open frame or sealed filterless blower motor.
Why is my Faber chimney making a loud humming noise without suction?
Loud humming usually happens when the motor run capacitor has weakened (below 2uF) or the blower fan wheel is jammed by hardened grease clumps. Replacing the capacitor or cleaning the fan restores suction.
Do you carry genuine compatible spare parts for Faber chimneys?
Yes, our technicians carry tested compatible Faber spare parts including baffle filters, push switches, capacitors, auto-clean coils, and touch display boards.
Why is oil dripping from the bottom of my Faber chimney?
Oil dripping happens when the internal collection channel is clogged or the baffle filter angle is misaligned. A thorough chamber clean and drain flush fixes this problem completely.
Can you install or realign the exhaust duct pipe for Faber chimneys?
Yes, we replace torn flexible aluminum ducts, realign bent duct runs, and fit sturdy external louvers with bird guards across Chennai.
How long does a Faber chimney repair visit take?
Most common repairs like capacitor replacement, push button repair, or auto-clean coil replacement take 45 to 60 minutes at your doorstep.
