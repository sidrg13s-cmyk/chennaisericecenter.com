Common Kaff Chimney Issues We Fix in Chennai

Kaff chimney owners across Chennai commonly encounter touch panel sensor glitches, high-speed blower imbalance, and auto-clean heating breaks.

🌪️ High-Capacity Suction Drop

Even powerful 1250+ m3/hr Kaff motors lose suction when heavy oil vapors coat the inner blower housing and block the exhaust backdraft damper.

🖐️ Gesture & Feather Touch Unresponsive

Optical sensors and capacitive touch panels on Kaff curved glass models fail to register finger taps due to steam moisture and grease films.

🔊 Blower Vibration & Rattle at High Speeds

Kaff high-RPM centrifugal blower wheels begin vibrating loudly when grease deposits accumulate unevenly across the curved plastic blades.

🔥 Dry Auto-Clean / Heat Coil Inactive

Heating element on Kaff auto-clean chimneys fails to generate heat, leaving the oil collector cup empty while grease stays trapped.

💧 Oil Dripping Around Base Trim

Grease leaks past the front stainless steel trim onto the kitchen slab when internal drainage channels become clogged with solidified oil.

⚡ PCB Board Error / Random Beeping

Voltage spikes in Chennai apartment complexes can damage the sensitive microprocessor on the Kaff main PCB, causing continuous beeps.

What is the visiting charge for Kaff chimney inspection in Chennai?

Why is my Kaff gesture control chimney not responding to hand gestures?
Cooking steam often creates a thin film of grease over the optical sensors behind the glass. Our technician cleans the sensors or replaces the sensor ribbon module if corroded.
How much does Kaff chimney deep cleaning cost in Chennai?
A complete Kaff chimney deep degreasing service ranges from ₹750 to ₹1,500 depending on model size (60cm vs 90cm) and the level of grease.
Can a humming Kaff chimney motor be repaired without replacement?
Yes. In most cases, a humming motor is caused by a failed capacitor or seized bearings, both of which can be replaced at your doorstep for a fraction of the cost of a new motor.
Why is oil dripping from the bottom edge of my Kaff chimney?
This occurs when the internal oil drainage channel gets blocked with thick grease. We clear the internal channel so oil drains properly into the collection cup.
Do you provide compatible spares for Kaff chimneys?
Yes, our technicians carry tested compatible Kaff spares including capacitors, baffle filters, oil trays, LED drivers, and touch PCBs.
Why is my Kaff chimney vibrating violently on speed 3?
High-speed vibration happens when grease accumulates unevenly on the blower fan wheel blades. A thorough chemical cleaning and rebalancing solves this.
How long does a Kaff chimney repair visit take?
