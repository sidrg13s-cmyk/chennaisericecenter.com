Frequently Asked Questions About Panasonic Repair in Chennai

Helpful answers regarding doorstep checking, repairs, spare parts, and visiting charges in Chennai.

Why is my Panasonic freezer making ice but the bottom food section is warm in Chennai?

Fridge cool aagala? In frost-free refrigerators, this classic symptom indicates that the defrost timer, bi-metal thermostat, or defrost heater has failed, blocking cold airflow to the lower compartment. Our Chennai technician inspects and replaces the faulty defrost part on-site.

Can gas recharging for a Panasonic refrigerator be done at home in Chennai?

Yes, our technician brings a portable vacuum pump, brazing torch, and refrigerant canister. We locate the leak point, braze it, replace the filter drier, pull a deep vacuum, and recharge refrigerant directly at your doorstep.

How do power cuts and voltage fluctuations in Chennai impact Panasonic inverter fridges?

Sudden voltage spikes can damage the inverter inverter board's switch-mode power supply. Our technicians test voltage regulation and install high-durability replacement driver boards.

Why does my Panasonic refrigerator make a clicking sound every few minutes?

A repeating click sound indicates that the compressor is trying to start but the PTC relay has failed or line voltage is fluctuating. The overload protector cuts off power to save the compressor. Replacing the relay fixes this immediately.

Do you service side-by-side and multi-door Panasonic refrigerators in Chennai?

Yes, we service single door direct cool, double door frost-free, and high-capacity side-by-side inverter refrigerators across all Chennai neighborhoods.

How much does a starter relay or thermostat replacement cost for a Panasonic fridge in Chennai?

Indicative pricing in Chennai: a PTC starter relay and overload protector costs roughly ₹450 to ₹950, while a mechanical thermostat replacement ranges from ₹650 to ₹1,250 depending on the model.

What is the warranty on replacement parts for Panasonic refrigerators?

All functional replacement spare parts—such as relays, fan motors, sensors, and thermostats—come with a 90-day service warranty for complete peace of mind.

Why is water pooling under the vegetable tray inside my Panasonic fridge?

This happens when the internal defrost drain tube gets choked with food debris or ice. Meltwater overflows into the lower crisping compartment instead of running to the rear evaporation pan. We clear the drain line cleanly.
