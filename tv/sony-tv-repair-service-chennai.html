Sony Bravia Red Light Blink Codes

Sony televisions incorporate an intelligent self-diagnostic system. When a fault occurs, the red standby LED blinks in a specific count:

  • 6 Blinks (Red LED): Backlight failure or backlight inverter circuit over-voltage / open circuit.
  • 4 Blinks (Red LED): Panel power supply error, T-Con DC-DC converter short, or LD board fault.
  • 2 Blinks (Red LED): Internal power supply board (SMPS) secondary DC voltage failure.
  • 5 Blinks (Red LED): T-Con timing controller communication failure or LVDS panel ribbon disconnection.

Note: Error messages and indicator codes can vary by model. Check the exact model number before assuming the fault.

TV Care and Regular Checking for Sony TVs

Sony televisions feature high-careful video processors and tightly calibrated backlight drivers. Regular cleaning around rear vents and maintaining steady voltage prevents thermal stress on the motherboard chipset and extends the operational life of your Bravia screen.

  • Keep adequate airflow around Sony Bravia rear heat sinks; avoid enclosing the TV tightly inside wooden cabinets.
  • Always disconnect the external 19.5V power adapter or power plug during intense thunderstorm activity in Chennai.
