Onida TV Standby & Indicator Light Warnings

Onida televisions indicate circuit status through the front standby LED sequences:

  • Fire TV Logo Bootloop: Amazon Fire OS system crash or corrupted eMMC storage.
  • Red Light Flashes 3 Times: Main power supply DC output voltage drop or short circuit.
  • Red Light Flashes 5 Times: Backlight inverter driver open circuit or LED strip burnout.
  • Continuous Red Flash: Panel communication error or T-Con board voltage regulator fault.

Note: Error messages and indicator codes can vary by model. Check the exact model number before assuming the fault.
