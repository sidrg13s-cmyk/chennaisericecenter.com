Common Lloyd AC Problems We Repair in Chennai

From sudden cooling drops and gas leaks to inverter PCB error codes, here are typical Lloyd faults our technicians fix at your doorstep.

❄️

Lloyd AC Blowing Warm Room Air

The indoor unit runs normally but air coming through the louvers lacks chillness. Usually caused by a blown run capacitor in non-inverter models or outdoor inverter PCB communication loss in Convertible units.

💧

Indoor Unit Water Leakage

Water drips down the painted wall or onto furniture. Caused by algae slime choking the condensate drain trough and drain hose. We clear the blockage with pressure water flushing.

🖥️

Lloyd Error Code Display (E1, E2, E4)

The digital display on the indoor panel flashes error codes indicating sensor failure, fan motor feedback stall, or refrigerant gas leakage. We check and find the exact circuit fault on-site.

⚡

Compressor Clicking & Tripping MCB

The outdoor unit hums, clicks, and trips the main electrical breaker. Caused by a weak capacitor, shorted fan motor winding, or low voltage supply across residential lines.

🧊

Ice Layer on Evaporator Cooling Coil

Frost covers the indoor aluminum fins, restricting airflow. Caused by fine dust clogging the filters or slow refrigerant loss from copper flare connections.

🔊

Outdoor Unit Excessive Vibration

Loud humming or vibrating noise resonates through the wall. Caused by hardened compressor rubber mounting pads or condenser fan blade imbalance.
