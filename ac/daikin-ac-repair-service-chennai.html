Common Daikin AC Problems We Repair in Chennai

From sudden cooling drops and gas leaks to inverter PCB error codes, here are typical Daikin faults our technicians fix at your doorstep.

❄️

Daikin AC Green Light Blinking (Error Trip)

The operation indicator on the indoor unit blinks steadily and the AC cuts off cooling. By holding the remote cancel button, we retrieve diagnostic fault codes (e.g., U4, A6, C4) to pinpoint the circuit issue.

💧

Daikin Indoor Unit Water Leaking

Water drips down the wall behind the indoor unit. Caused by a clogged drain pan outlet, algae slime in the drain hose, or improper back-plate level alignment.

🖥️

U4 Communication Error Between Units

The outdoor unit fails to start because signal communication between indoor and outdoor PCBs is interrupted. We check interconnecting control wiring and outdoor inverter board power circuits.

🌀

Outdoor Unit Fan Stopped Running

The outdoor fan motor stalls, causing condenser temperature to rise rapidly until the compressor trips on thermal protection. We replace faulty outdoor DC fan motors and capacitors.

📉

Daikin Refrigerant Gas Leak (C4/EC Code)

Gradual cooling drop over weeks with oil marks on copper flare joints. We perform nitrogen pressure leak testing, silver brazing, deep vacuumization, and exact R32 gas recharging.

↔️

Coanda Flap Not Opening / Motor Stripped

The motorized horizontal airflow louver fails to open or remains stuck midway. Caused by a stripped stepper motor gear. We install fresh Daikin louver stepper motors.

Note: Fault code nomenclature can vary slightly between inverter series and production years. Our local technicians carry multi-meter testing gear to verify thermistors, communication loops, and power lines directly before replacing components.

Error Code Possible Issue What Our Technician Checks
U4 Communication error between indoor unit and outdoor inverter unit Check inter-unit communication cable (terminal 3), outdoor PCB DC voltages, and fuse.
A6 Indoor blower fan motor lock or speed feedback error Check cross-flow blower free rotation, motor harness connector, and indoor PCB fan driver.
C4 Liquid pipe heat exchanger thermistor failure Test copper pipe temperature sensor resistance; replace defective thermistor probe.
C9 Suction air temperature thermistor open or shorted Inspect room ambient thermistor on front grille; verify resistance curve and replace.

A3 Drain level water level alarm (Cassette AC) Inspect condensate lift pump operation, check float switch continuity, and clear drain line.

Daikin AC Gas / Refrigerant Service in Chennai

Daikin air conditioners rely heavily on pure R32 refrigerant. Because R32 operates at high operating pressures, flare connections at outdoor service valves must be torqued precisely. If your Daikin AC loses cooling, our technicians perform nitrogen leak testing at 250 PSI, re-flare copper lines using eccentric flaring tools, evacuate with a two-stage vacuum pump down to 500 microns, and charge R32 by exact gram weight.

🔍 Nitrogen Leak Test High-pressure nitrogen testing to locate hairline pinholes on cooling coils and flare nuts.
🌀 Deep Vacuuming Evacuating moisture and non-condensables from copper pipelines using a dedicated vacuum pump.
