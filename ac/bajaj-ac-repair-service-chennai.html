Common Bajaj AC Problems We Repair in Chennai

From sudden cooling drops and gas leaks to inverter PCB error codes, here are typical Bajaj faults our technicians fix at your doorstep.

❄️

Weak Airflow & Insufficient Cooling

Air coming from the louvers feels lukewarm or weak. Usually caused by dust-choked cooling pads or a failing run capacitor in the motor circuit.

💧

Water Overflowing from Base Tank

Water spills over floors from the bottom sump. Caused by a stuck float valve, cracked water tank, or misaligned drainage plug.

⚡

Motor Humming & Tripping Breaker

The unit hums loudly without spinning the fan blade and trips household MCBs. Indicates a seized motor bearing or shorted capacitor.

🔊

Severe Vibration & Rattling Noise

Loud rattling sounds caused by loose motor mounting bolts or unbalanced fan blades. We balance the fan wheel and secure the chassis.

👃

Foul Odor from Damp Cooling Media

Stale water in the bottom reservoir breeds algae and releases unpleasant odors into the room. We sanitize the water tank and clean the pads.

🖥️

Remote Control & Display Panel Unresponsive

Digital display lights do not turn on or ignore remote commands due to moisture damage on the PCB receiver board.

Can Bajaj inverter AC outdoor PCB boards be repaired at home in Chennai?
