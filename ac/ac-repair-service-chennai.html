AC Not Cooling at All The indoor unit blows room temperature air without chillness. Often caused by a burnt capacitor, failed compressor start relay, low refrigerant pressure from a gas leak, or outdoor unit power failure.

AC Not Switching On / No Power The unit does not respond to the remote or manual button, and the display panel remains blank. Points to a blown PCB fuse, burnt transformer, faulty power socket, or damaged input wiring.
🌀
Outdoor Unit Not Working The indoor unit runs but the outdoor compressor and condenser fan do not turn on. Caused by wiring disconnection, weak dual capacitor, inverter IPM power board fault, or high-pressure cut-off.
💧
Water Leaking from Indoor Unit Water drips down the wall, onto the bed, or pools on the floor. Caused by a clogged condensate drain pipe, cracked drain pan, algae slime buildup, or tilted indoor unit mounting.
🔊
Unusual Rattling or Humming Noise Loud vibrations, squeaking, or grinding noises from indoor or outdoor units. Caused by loose cabinet screws, misaligned blower wheel, dry motor bearings, or worn compressor rubber mounts.
📱
AC Remote Not Working / Sensor Issue The AC fails to receive remote signals even after replacing batteries. Usually caused by a defective infrared receiver eye on the indoor display board or stuck sensor pins.
💨
Indoor Blower Fan Stopped Cooling coil freezes up because the cross-flow blower is not spinning to circulate air. Indicates a burnt blower motor, shorted fan capacitor, or seized bushing.
🔄
Compressor Not Starting / Tripping MCB The outdoor compressor makes a faint hum and clicks off, or immediately trips the household MCB breaker. Indicates a defective run capacitor, shorted motor winding, or low line voltage.
⏱️
AC Frequently Switching Off (Short Cycling) The AC runs for 3 to 5 minutes and shuts off before cooling the room. Usually caused by a faulty room temperature thermistor, dirty condenser coil overheating, or low refrigerant gas.
🧊
Ice Formation on Indoor Coil & Copper Pipe Thick frost forms on the thin copper pipe or front cooling coil. Caused by choked airflow through dirty filters or low refrigerant pressure from a copper joint leak.
👃
Bad Smell / Musty Odor from AC Airflow A sour or damp odor spreads across the room when the AC starts. Caused by bacterial slime, mold, and dust accumulation inside the wet drain tray and blower wheel. Needs foam deep jet cleaning.
📉
Refrigerant Gas Leakage Gradual cooling drop over weeks with oil residue visible around copper flare joints. We perform nitrogen pressure leak testing, brazing repair, vacuumization, and precise gas charging.
🖥️
PCB Fault & Error Code Flashing The display panel shows error codes (e.g., E1, E6, F3) or the timer light blinks repeatedly. We provide circuit-level repair and board replacement for inverter and non-inverter models.

Inverter AC Repair Service in Chennai

Need inverter AC repair in Chennai? Modern inverter air conditioners use variable-speed BLDC compressors and modern electronic controller boards (PCBs) to regulate room temperature smoothly while reducing monthly power consumption. However, because inverter systems rely heavily on sensitive microcontrollers, voltage fluctuations and ambient humidity can cause circuit faults.

When an inverter AC malfunctions, it typically flashes an error code on the indoor LED display or stops cooling without warning. Common faults include communication loss between indoor and outdoor PCB modules, shorted IPM (Intelligent Power Module) drive transistors, defective ambient/tube thermistors, or seized electronic expansion valves (EEVs). Unlike conventional ACs, inverter systems need precise multimeter checks, component-level testing, and exact refrigerant gram weighing during gas recharge. Our technicians are trained to check inverter error codes accurately and perform board repairs directly at your premises whenever possible.

Common Inverter AC Solutions We Provide:

Looking for dependable non-inverter AC service in Chennai? Fixed-speed non-inverter air conditioners feature sturdy, straightforward electrical circuits that have kept Chennai families cool for decades. Why a Working AC Matters in Chennai

Chennai experiences a tropical local climate characterized by high temperatures and persistent humidity throughout most of the year. When an air conditioner stops working, indoor spaces quickly turn stuffy and suffocating. High humidity prevents sweat from evaporating naturally, making restful night sleep nearly impossible and leaving family members exhausted the next morning. For families with young children, infants, or elderly parents, an uncomfortably hot room causes restlessness and skin irritation. Furthermore, for people working from home or managing customer-facing offices and clinics in Chennai, a working air conditioner is essential to maintain daily productivity, comfort, and focus.

Why Regular AC Service Is Important

Because Chennai is situated close to the coast, airborne dust and salty moisture continuously pass through your air conditioner's indoor cooling coils and outdoor condenser fins. Over months of regular use, dirt builds up on air filters and restricts essential airflow. When airflow is choked, the cooling coil freezes into solid ice, water drips onto floors, and the compressor is forced to run continuously under high discharge pressure, leading to steep electricity bills and premature motor burnout. Routine servicing and seasonal jet cleaning remove dirt, clear clogged drain channels, protect copper coils from salt corrosion, and allow technicians to spot weak capacitors or slow gas leaks before a sudden cooling breakdown occurs.

Why is my air conditioner AC blowing warm room air without chilling in Chennai?

AC cooling kammiya irukka? In Chennai's humid weather, cooling drops usually occur when the outdoor condenser fins get clogged with dust, or when refrigerant gas leaks from a flared copper pipe joint. Why is water leaking continuously from my air conditioner indoor AC unit?

Water dripping inside the bedroom usually happens when the indoor condensate drain channel or flexible discharge hose gets blocked with algae and fine dirt. Our technician clears the drain pipe with a pressurized flush and verifies proper unit tilt.
