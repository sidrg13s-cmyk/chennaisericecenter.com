Common Customer Service Experiences

Real Doorstep Service Examples – typical customer complaints handled by our technicians for Home Appliance across Chennai.

Inverter Split AC (1.5 Ton) ⭐ 10/10

Complaint: "AC running full day but room-la temperature kuraiyala, warm air thaan varuthu."

Technician Checked: Refrigerant suction pressure, outdoor condenser coil airflow, and dual run capacitor.

Fault & Fix: Outdoor condenser fins choked with coastal dust and micro-leak near service flare nut. Tightened brass flare joint, performed vacuum hold test, and recharged eco-friendly gas.

Approx. Cost: ₹1,450 – ₹2,600 📍 Senneerkuppam
Window AC (1.5 Ton) ⭐ 10/10

Complaint: "Outdoor side-la irundhu heavy metal rattling and shaking sound varuthu."

Technician Checked: Blower fan blade balance, compressor rubber anti-vibration grommets, and chassis mounts.

Fault & Fix: Aged, cracked rubber mounting bushes caused metal chassis resonance. Installed new heavy-duty rubber vibration dampeners and tightened motor mount bolts.

Approx. Cost: ₹600 – ₹1,200 📍 St. Thomas Mount
Front Load Washing Machine (7 Kg) ⭐ 9/10

Complaint: "Wash cycle mudinjathum door open aagala, door handle stuck aaiduchu."

Technician Checked: Bi-metal PTC door interlock switch, door latch hook, and PCB lock output.

Fault & Fix: Bi-metal heater element inside door lock switch failed in locked position. Safely unlocked door, installed OEM door interlock switch, and tested latch release.

Approx. Cost: ₹750 – ₹1,450 📍 George Town
Side-by-Side Inverter Refrigerator (580L) ⭐ 8/10

Complaint: "Food items spoiling quickly, control panel temp setting change panna mudiyala."

Technician Checked: Inverter inverter drive PCB board, DC fan motor, and interior thermistor probes.

Fault & Fix: Power surge damaged the inverter driver board low-voltage supply line. Replaced blown surge suppressor and filter capacitors on motherboard; restored precision cooling.

Approx. Cost: ₹1,500 – ₹3,200 📍 Pallikaranai
Front Load Washing Machine (6 Kg) ⭐ 10/10

Complaint: "Rubber gasket torn aachu, front panel vazhiya water spill aaguthu."

Technician Checked: Door boot rubber gasket (bellow), clamp spring ring, and drain lip.

Fault & Fix: Sharp coin cut a tear in the lower fold of the rubber door bellow. Fitted new high-grade synthetic rubber door boot gasket and tensioned outer steel retaining clamp.

Approx. Cost: ₹1,200 – ₹2,400 📍 Kilpauk
Semi Automatic Twin Tub (7.5 Kg) ⭐ 10/10

Complaint: "Spin tub-la clothes pota rotate aagala, motor hum pannitu stop aaguthu."

Technician Checked: Spin motor run capacitor, safety lid brake cable mechanism, and motor windings.

Fault & Fix: Weak dual capacitor and rusted mechanical safety brake holding the spin shaft. Replaced motor run capacitor, lubricated brake linkage assembly, and tested drying spin.

Approx. Cost: ₹550 – ₹1,150 📍 Alandur
